# Release notes

## v0.1.3 (2026-11-20)

Resubmission package for *JCIM* (manuscript ci-2026-041167). Adds the ligand
electron-density screen requested by the editor and documents the construction
criteria that were previously implicit in the code.

Added
- `code/build_svhop_benchmark.py --edia 0.8`: a pair is admitted only when both
  ligands have EDIA_m >= 0.8. The gate is on by default; `--no-edia` rebuilds the
  analysed set. Every pair in `dataset/pdb_hops/benchmark.json` now also carries
  `ediam1`, `ediam2`, `edia_min` and `edia_pass`.
- `code/fetch_edia.py` and `code/analyze_edia.py`: ProteinsPlus EDIA REST client
  (two-phase submit/poll, cached per entry) and the audit/sensitivity analysis.
- `dataset/edia/`: `edia_ligands.csv` (528 instances, EDIA_m + copy count + status),
  `edia_status.csv` (522 entries: 503 scored, 19 without structure factors),
  `edia_pairs.csv` (per pair), `edia_failures.csv` (164 ligands below the gate or
  unscorable), `edia_summary.json` (counts and sensitivity).
- Supporting Information Tables S10 (35-accession target panel), S11 (EDIA audit
  and EDIA-restricted sensitivity) and S12 (ligands failing the gate), now also
  generated by `code/make_si.py`.
- `reproduce.sh` steps 0b (EDIA table), 1 (analysed and gated benchmarks) and
  8 (audit).

Changed
- `benchmark.json` keeps its ids, pair order and splits: it is the set analysed in
  the manuscript (2,886 pairs), assembled before the gate existed. The gate is
  therefore applied as an audit and the evaluations are repeated on the passing
  subset (Tables S11/S12); the gated set is written to `benchmark_edia08.json`.
- `README.md`, `CITATION.cff`, `dataset/README.md` updated for v0.1.3.

## v0.1.2 (2026-10-28)

Manuscript repositioned as a benchmark paper and prepared for *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling*.

Added
- `code/svhop_diversity_control.py` and `artifacts/target_holdout/equal_diversity.json`:
  per-pair scaffold counting and rarefaction to equal diversity.
- SI Table S9; manuscript section 3.8.1.

Corrected
- The per-family joint top-1 in the manuscript was carrying the property-arm rates; the
  SV-Hop rates are 57.8 (kinase), 79.1 (enzyme_other), 50.0 (protease), 63.3
  (nuclear_receptor), 70.0 (gpcr); overall 62.1% (185/298).
- The lexicon claim: candidate diversity explains about one third of the PDB-only advantage
  (18.9 -> 13.1 pp), not all of it; rule composition accounts for the rest.
- Rule-count definitions are now stated explicitly (60,069 / 54,123 / 4,029).
- SI S6 CI harmonised to [60.3, 70.0]; per-family summary rows added.
